# Statistical-Simulation-Petros-Dellaportas-SpSe-2025-AUEB-
This reepository is consisted of the three most challenging problems, that professor Dellaportas set us during his class about Statistical Simulation Techniques.

**Problem 1**: Use the rejection sampling method for creating samples from Beta(1.6,2.1) with acceptance probability greater than 90%

**Probem 2**: We want to evaluate the risk of bankruptcy for an imaginary country with complex fiscal challenges, “Grecontina”. The combined risk of bankruptcy is modeled using a Delaporte distribution, which captures both the systematic volatility (Negative Binomial) and random shocks(Poisson). The goal is to simulate bankruptcy risk data and estimate the parameters of the underlying distribution to inform policy recommendations.

**Problem 3**: (i) Examine the operation and general functionality of three MetropolisHastings algorithms (RW Metropolis, Metropolis Adjusted Langevile Algorithm, Gaussian Invariant MALA). (ii) How the well-known Control Variate reduction technique is applied in GI-MALA.
